# Writing a system page

A system shows several strategies working as one treasury. The value is in how the parts relate, not in the parts themselves.

**Open with what the whole thing is for.** One line. Which reader is this the right shape for?

**Show the full allocation.** Every part of the capital, with amounts and states, adding up to the total. Include running costs — a system page that ignores fees is incomplete.

**Draw the flow.** Where money arrives, where it goes, what returns. A small diagram usually beats a paragraph.

**Explain running it.** What needs checking monthly, what needs a decision, what happens on its own.

**Say how to grow into it, or out of it.** What's the simpler version, and what's the next step up?

**End with what to watch for.** Including the failure specific to combining these particular strategies, not just generic ones.

## Voice

Systems pages are read by someone deciding whether to commit a real treasury. Be concrete about what it costs to run and what it demands of them.

Name the temptation. Most system failures are someone unlocking a reserve during a bad month. Say so.

Link down to the strategies rather than re-explaining them. This page is about the combination.

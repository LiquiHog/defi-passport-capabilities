# NFT project treasury

Turning a mint into a project that still exists in two years.

A collection sells out and a large amount arrives at once. What happens in the following month decides whether the roadmap gets delivered or quietly abandoned. The hard part is that mint day feels like the end of the work, when it's the start of the funding problem.

## The allocation

A collection raising 200,000 USDC, with an 18-month roadmap costing about 8,000 a month:

| Purpose | Amount | State |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery runway — 18 months | 144,000 | **Locked**, paid out on schedule |
| Long-term treasury | 30,000 | [Rebalancing](../strategies/portfolio/rebalancing.md), 50/50 |
| Earning | 20,000 | [Idle capital lending](../strategies/lending/idle-capital.md) |
| Buffer and running costs | 6,000 | Fees, minimum balance, the unexpected |

Locking the runway first is the decision that matters. It isn't a promise in a Discord post — it's capital the project genuinely cannot spend on something else in month three.

## Paying the team

Fund [recurring payments](../use-cases/commerce/recurring-payments.md) from the locked runway: a fixed amount, to a known address, every month, with an end date. Contributors can verify their payment stream exists, which is worth more to a project's credibility than any announcement.

## Royalties arriving later

Royalty income is irregular and unpredictable, which makes it bad at funding fixed costs. Treat it as a separate stream: top up the runway first, then split what's left between the treasury and the team. See [royalty revenue](../use-cases/commerce/nft/royalty-revenue.md).

## What to watch for

**Runway is the whole game.** Most failed projects had the money and spent it on the wrong things first. Lock 18 months before allocating anything to a treasury strategy.

**Mint revenue arrives once.** Unless royalties are substantial and reliable, this is the capital the project has. Plan as though nothing else is coming.

**Don't hold the treasury in your own token.** If the project struggles, the token falls at exactly the moment you need the treasury — and you have no runway and no reserve.

**Publish the structure.** Holders can see the passport's allocations. A locked, verifiable runway is a much stronger signal than a roadmap graphic.

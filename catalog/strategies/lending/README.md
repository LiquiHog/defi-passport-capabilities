# Lending strategies

Earning on capital you are not using, and raising cash without selling.

| | |
|---|---|
| **[Idle capital](idle-capital.md)** | Lend the surplus, keep the runway liquid |
| **[Borrowing](borrowing.md)** | Raise cash against what you hold instead of selling it |
| **[Lending plus trading](lending-plus-trading.md)** | A lending allocation alongside live trading inventory |
| **[Lending plus rebalancing](lending-plus-rebalancing.md)** | Earn on the surplus while the liquid part holds its mix |
| **[Capital recycling](capital-recycling.md)** | Move capital between lending, reserves and strategies as needs change |

Capital in a lending protocol follows that protocol's rules, not the passport's. See [lending and borrowing](../../../docs/advanced/lending-and-capital-use.md).

# Trading strategies

Rules that buy and sell for you, from an inventory you cap up front.

**[Basic](basic/README.md)** — one rule, one job. A grid, a weekly buy, a sell order. Any of these is a complete strategy on its own. Or [swap on demand](basic/swap.md) without a rule at all.

**[Advanced](advanced/README.md)** — shaping a single idea. Uneven grids, layered price regions, deliberate buying curves.

**[Composed](composed/README.md)** — two strategies side by side, each with its own money.

Every trade carries a worst-acceptable price. If the market cannot fill you at that price or better, the trade does not happen — see [how automation works](../../../docs/overview/how-automation-works.md).

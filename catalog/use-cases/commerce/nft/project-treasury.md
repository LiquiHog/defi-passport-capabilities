# NFT project treasury

Funding the work that's promised while managing what's left for the years after.

Once a mint is allocated and the team is paid, a project still has a treasury to run — and a different problem to the one it had on mint day. This is about the steady state.

## The two jobs

**Delivery** is funded, scheduled and locked. It should be boring: known costs, known dates, no decisions.

**The long-term treasury** is what outlives the roadmap. It's smaller, it can take some risk, and it's what funds whatever the project does next.

| Purpose | Amount | State |
|---|---|---|
| Remaining delivery runway | 80,000 | **Locked**, scheduled payments |
| Long-term treasury | 35,000 | [Rebalancing](../../../strategies/portfolio/rebalancing.md), 50/50 |
| Earning | 15,000 | [Lending](../../../strategies/lending/idle-capital.md) |
| Working capital | 10,000 | Available |

## Keeping them separate

The separation is the point. A locked delivery runway can't be borrowed from for an opportunity, and the treasury can't be raided when delivery runs over budget.

When delivery does run over — it usually does — that's a decision to make explicitly: extend the runway from the treasury, reduce scope, or find revenue. All three are better than discovering the money went quietly.

## Adapting it

Route royalty income through the same order every time: runway first, then treasury. See [royalty revenue](royalty-revenue.md).

Shift the balance as delivery completes. A project six months from finishing its roadmap needs less locked runway and can grow the long-term treasury.

Fund a community allocation as its own line, so grants and bounties don't come out of delivery.

## What to watch for

**Delivery always costs more than planned.** Build that in rather than discovering it. A runway with no slack is a runway that ends early.

**The treasury is not delivery contingency.** If it becomes the overflow account, you have one pot again and no long-term treasury.

**Holders can see all of this.** A verifiable split between funded delivery and long-term treasury is a genuine credibility advantage. Use it.

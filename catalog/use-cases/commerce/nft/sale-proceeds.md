# NFT sale proceeds

Mint day brings in the money. The next fortnight decides whether the roadmap happens.

A collection sells out and a large amount arrives at once. It feels like an ending; it's the start of an eighteen-month funding problem. Projects that fail usually had the money and allocated it badly in the first month.

## The allocation

A collection raising 200,000 USDC against an 18-month roadmap costing 8,000 a month:

| Purpose | Amount | State |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery runway | 144,000 | **Locked**, paid out on schedule |
| Long-term treasury | 30,000 | [Rebalancing](../../../strategies/portfolio/rebalancing.md) |
| Earning | 20,000 | [Idle capital lending](../../../strategies/lending/idle-capital.md) |
| Buffer | 6,000 | Available |

Lock the runway first, before anything else is decided. It stops the treasury being spent on a good idea in month three.

## Paying the team

Fund [recurring payments](../recurring-payments.md) from the locked runway — fixed amounts, known addresses, monthly, with an end date.

This is worth more than it sounds. Contributors can verify the funding exists. Holders can see the project is funded for eighteen months. Both are stronger signals than any announcement.

## Adapting it

Convert volatile proceeds to something stable before locking the runway. Runway denominated in a volatile asset is not runway.

Treat [royalties](royalty-revenue.md) as a separate, unpredictable stream — top up the runway first, then split what's left.

Extend the runway from royalty income rather than increasing spending.

## What to watch for

**Mint revenue arrives once.** Unless royalties are reliable and substantial, this is the money the project has. Plan as though nothing else is coming, because usually nothing is.

**Don't hold the treasury in your own token.** If the project struggles, the token falls exactly when the treasury is needed.

**Spending rises to meet available capital.** A locked runway with scheduled payments is a constraint that protects the project from its own optimism.

**Publish the structure.** A verifiable eighteen-month runway is the most credible roadmap signal available to you.
